FAQ

Before you
run.

When do we meet?

Every Sunday morning at 6:30 AM.

Where do we meet?

We meet at BM Habitat Mall.

What is the route?

We start at BM Habitat Mall, run towards Chalwamba Park, turn around and return. The target distance is 5K.

Do I have to be fast?

Nope. Sunday Miles is about running together and enjoying the community.

Can beginners join?

Absolutely. Run at a comfortable pace and build up gradually.

What should I bring?

Comfortable running shoes, water and whatever you normally need for a morning run.
